Windows port of [GBO-Code-Fairy-PS4RT](https://github.com/HekamiAeon/GBO-Code-Fairy-PS4RT), upstream `main` at `0252c9d` (v1.0 plus two commits).

- Unzip, then open `SiegZeon.exe`. The game must be your own extracted CUSA28763 folder. The 01.03 update and the three volume keys stay PKG files: the launcher installs them.
- The launcher is in English, with French on the EN button.
- Crowbar compatible (`crowbar.json`): Crowbar can start it and records trophies.
- New in 1.0.1: the Guide button of [Crowbar](https://github.com/DiamondDogz/crowbar) pauses the game (crowbar-sdk 1.2): picture, sound and timers stop while its menu is open. During a movie, Crowbar says the game cannot pause yet.
- See [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/DiamondDogz/GBO-Code-Fairy-PS4RT-Windows/blob/windows/INSTALL.md).
